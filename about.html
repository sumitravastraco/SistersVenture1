About Sumitra Vastra & Co.

At Sumitra Vastra & Co., we celebrate the beauty of tradition while embracing the ease of modern living. Our brand was created with a simple vision – to design clothing that feels as good as it looks. Every piece is a blend of heritage, comfort, and timeless elegance, crafted for women who believe everyday wear should make them feel special. Inspired by cultural roots and crafted with care, Sumitra Vastra & Co. is not just about clothing – it’s about weaving together stories, emotions, and style into every outfit.

Our journey is guided by the vision, creativity, and dedication of our co-founders

Pritika Pundir

Operations & Quality Head, dedicated to maintaining premium quality and a seamless customer experience.

Suhani Thakur

Marketing & Branding Lead, experienced in fashion campaigns and building meaningful connections with our community.

Yachna Pundir

Product Designer, blending heritage patterns with contemporary style to create timeless, elegant pieces.

Nitika Pundir

Creative Director, passionate about sustainable fabrics and traditional weaving, ensuring each collection honors cultural heritage.

Together, they inspire every creation at Sumitra Vastra & Co., combining tradition, innovation, and care to make each outfit a celebration of style and confidence.
